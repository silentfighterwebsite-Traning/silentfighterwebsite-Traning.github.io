The academy

A quiet room. A loud work ethic.

Our name comes from the idea that a fighter doesn't need to announce anything. You show up, you drill the basics until they are clean, and you let the mat do the talking.

  • DisciplineOn time, gloves wrapped, ready to work.
  • TechniqueStance, footwork and clean strikes before power.
  • RespectFor coaches, partners and the mat. Every round ends with a glove touch.

Training

Pick your lane

Every class starts the same way: warm-up, drills, pad work, then conditioning. What changes is the pace and the goal.

Kids

A first taste of kickboxing built around balance, coordination and confidence.

  • Stance and footwork games
  • Basic punches and kicks on pads
  • Listening, patience, teamwork

Juniors & teens

More volume and more detail. Combinations, defence, timing and fitness.

  • Jab, cross, hook, low and high kicks
  • Blocking, checking and counters
  • Strength and stamina circuits

Competition team

For students who want to compete. Sparring with gear, ring rules and match prep.

  • Controlled sparring rounds
  • Tournament preparation
  • Medals earned on the mat

Class times and fees are shared directly by the academy. Message us on Instagram and we will reply with what fits your child or you.
